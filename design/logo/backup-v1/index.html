Vision Menu · Brand Mark

The VM Monogram

A monoline geometric monogram — one stroke weight, flat terminals, circle-and-straight construction — carried over from the RemCall “RC-CUT” system and re-cut as VM in the Bordeaux Noir palette. Wine leads; champagne gold is the accent; warm neutrals carry the type.

Primary lockup

Mark + wordmark, cap-aligned. Wordmark set in Inter Bold (Vision Menu’s companion grotesque) to echo the RemCall lockup. Vision in ink/off-white, Menu in wine to tie back to the mark. Outline the text to paths at production.
